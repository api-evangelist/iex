# IEX (Investors Exchange)

> IEX Group, Inc. operates IEX Exchange, a U.S. national securities exchange for equities (with IEX Options coming soon), dedicated to fairer, more transparent trading. Its developer-facing surface is market-data and connectivity: real-time depth-of-book and top-of-book feeds (DEEP+, DEEP, TOPS) distributed over the binary IEX Transport Protocol (IEX-TP), plus downloadable historical PCAP samples and published feed specifications. Access is via a Data Subscriber Agreement rather than a public REST API.

## Products
- [IEX Exchange](https://www.iex.io/): Equities trading venue featuring the Speed Bump, IEX Signal, and the D-Limit order type.
- [IEX Options](https://www.iex.io/): Options exchange (coming soon) focused on helping liquidity providers manage adverse selection.

## Market Data & Connectivity
- [Market Data](https://www.iex.io/resources/trading/market-data): DEEP+ (v1.02), DEEP+ SNAP (v1.03), DEEP (v1.08), DEEP SNAP (v1.5), TOPS (v1.66), TOPS SNAP (v1.5) feeds over IEX-TP v1, with sample PCAP files.
- [Connect Directly](https://www.iex.io/resources/trading/get-connected-directly): Direct connectivity to IEX.
- [Third Party Partners Connection](https://www.iex.io/resources/trading/third-party-partners-connection): Access via extranet / market-data partners.
- [Market Stats](https://iextrading.com/stats/): Public market statistics.
